### 💥 #10 — Xenakis 把交通流写成了乐谱：城市是随机的，但谱面是死的

**锚点**: iannis-xenakis.org《Stochastic Music》（Pithoprakta 1955–56 第 52–59 小节的图示对照；"In the wake of Pithoprakta, Xenakis developed the idea of total indeterminism"；"What is the minimum of logical constraints necessary to produce a piece of music"）/ 旁证：Wikipedia「The Art of Noises」（Russolo 1913 与助手 Ugo Piatti 在米兰工作室造出 Intonarumori 噪声乐器族，并为它们写了 en-harmonic notation 记谱，"all other noises are only associations and combinations of these"）；arXiv:1112.3761（自适应信号灯下干道路网的宏观基本图）
